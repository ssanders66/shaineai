“These are real songs about real things. I just want you to hear them.”

Shaine is a rock artist blending the raw emotion of classic and alternative rock with modern production and technology. Drawing inspiration from artists ranging from Pink Floyd and The Black Crowes to Soundgarden, Shaine builds songs around powerful guitars, memorable melodies, and honest storytelling.

Rather than chasing a single genre or era, Shaine explores the different sides of rock — gritty, melodic, heavy, atmospheric, and sometimes deeply personal. At the center of it all is a simple philosophy:
